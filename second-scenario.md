# Second scenario

The second scenario was intended to test a shorter time limit and a different activity preference.

Inputs used:
- Available time: 10 minutes
- Environment: an ordinary urban neighbourhood in Bengaluru
- Budget: zero
- Equipment: none
- Activity preference: mindful walking
- Experience level: beginner

The original prompt structure was reused, changing the available time and activity preference.

## Observed output

**Quest title:** The Concrete Jungle Safari

**Mission:** Discover three small signs of nature thriving within your immediate urban surroundings.

**Three activities:**
1. Look up at the nearest tree and observe the pattern of its branches against the sky.
2. Stand still for 30 seconds and identify one sound made by a bird or insect.
3. Find a plant or patch of moss growing in an unexpected place, such as a wall crack or pavement gap.

**Observation to record:** After returning, write down or sketch the most interesting shape you saw today.

**Safety reminder:** Stay on the sidewalk and remain mindful of passing traffic.

## Note

Although the output is practical, it reads more like a general nature-exploration quest than a clearly mindful-walking-specific quest. It also does not state an estimated duration. This is evidence of partial rather than perfect personalization.
